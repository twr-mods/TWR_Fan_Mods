All Epilogues Revolution Path (Fan-Made)

Narrator
With the old order broken and Fern’s capital reclaimed, the revolution finally came to an end. Yet victory did not bring peace overnight. From the ashes of war, the people of Fern began the long and painful work of building a country that belonged to them at last.

Slyker & Illyana - The Ones Who Moved Forward
Slyker refused every title offered to him, claiming he had only ever been a Ranger with too much luck. Still, his voice carried far among the people, and many looked to him when Fern’s new path began to take shape.
Illyana remained at his side through the uncertain years that followed. Though the scars of her past never fully faded, she became a symbol of Fern’s wounded people and the future they had fought to reclaim.
Together, they were remembered not as rulers, but as the two who chose to keep moving forward when Fern itself had forgotten how.

Arland & Abigail - Shield and Arrow
After the revolution, Arland laid down his old oath and devoted himself to protecting the villages Fern had forgotten. He never sought command again, but wherever danger threatened the weak, his shield was the first to stand in its way.
Abigail returned to the wild paths around her village, bow in hand and herbs at her belt. Though her remedies remained valued, it was her skill as a hunter that kept many hungry homes alive through Fern’s hardest winters.
Arland was often seen at her side, silent as stone while she tracked game, guarded roads, and drove off those who still preyed on common folk. In time, the shield and the arrow became a familiar sight across Fern’s recovering lands.

Phoebe & Douglas - The Lady Who Chose Her Life
Phoebe never returned to the life once chosen for her. She used her name, wealth, and healing arts to aid the poor, the wounded, and those left homeless by the war.
Many expected her to eventually retreat into comfort, but Phoebe had already found the life she wished to live. She remained among the people she had once run away to understand.
Douglas stayed at her side, refusing every post that would take him elsewhere. To him, protecting the life Phoebe had chosen was duty enough.

Reyson - Shadow’s Blade
Reyson vanished before the victory banners were raised. For years after, rumors followed him: corrupt officers exposed, slavers cut down, and old loyalists found fleeing from shadows that moved like steel.
No one could prove the stories true. But wherever Fern’s darkness grew too deep, people whispered that Shadow’s Blade was still at work.

Elias - The Inheritor’s Burden
Elias helped rebuild the Rangers, this time refusing to hide the doubts he had once seen as weakness. He taught younger recruits that duty without question could become just another chain.
Though he never fully escaped his father’s shadow, he no longer lived beneath it. In time, he became the kind of leader he had once feared he could never be.

Cedric - The Gentle Guide
Cedric spent the years after the revolution tending to the wounded and comforting the grieving. His chapel welcomed soldiers from both sides of the war, asking only that they leave their hatred at the door.
Many came carrying weapons. Most left without them.

Kaelith & Cassidy - The Bandwagon’s Lifeline
After the revolution, Kaelith reshaped the Bandwagon into something Fern’s broken roads desperately needed: a lifeline. What had once survived through theft and secrecy became a network of shelters, hidden routes, and helping hands for those the new order had not yet reached.
She claimed she had gone respectable, though those who threatened her people often discovered that Kaelith’s idea of persuasion still left bruises.
Cassidy remained at her side, silent as ever, serving not from debt alone, but from loyalty freely chosen. Together, they gave the Bandwagon something greater than survival — they gave it a future.

Melanie - The Accidental Revolutionary
Melanie had once tried to steal a dusty old book and somehow ended up helping topple a regime. Even after facing blood, death, and battles she was certain she would sneak away from, she stayed with the Rangers until the very end.
She never admitted how deeply their stubborn hope had moved her.
After the revolution, Melanie returned to her old habits, drifting from town to town and relieving the wealthy of things they clearly did not need. From time to time, her little “excursions” crossed paths with Kaelith and Cassidy’s work, usually with suspiciously full purses appearing wherever the Bandwagon needed them most.
Whether she had become generous or simply more selective with her targets, Melanie refused to say.

Benjen & Rho - Wesley’s Line
Benjen and Rho continued serving under General Wesley, helping transform Fern’s army from a weapon of fear into a shield for the people.
Their names rarely appeared in songs, but every soldier knew the story of the line they refused to surrender. In the new army, “standing like Benjen and Rho” became a saying for holding firm against impossible odds.

Sven & Hilda - Children of the Fallen
After the revolution, Sven and Hilda returned to the land where their father had been taken from them, and where the viking who ruined their family had left his scars.
Sven honored his father by throwing himself into danger wherever the weak needed protecting, laughing loudly enough to hide the grief he still carried.
Hilda chose a quieter path, tending the wounded and keeping death at bay with the same stubbornness that had kept her brother alive. Together, they rebuilt what had been stolen from them - not as it once was, but as something their father would have been proud to see.

Finn - The Son Who Moved On
Finn returned to his father’s grave after the war and finally stood before it without lowering his eyes. His grief never vanished, but it no longer held him still.
He later became a quiet but trusted Ranger, known less for glory than for always taking the next step forward.

Ajax - The World-Shaker
Ajax never adjusted well to peace. After the revolution, he traveled from town to town, crushing bandit gangs, old loyalist cells, and occasionally tavern furniture.
Despite his chaos, he was loved by the common folk. Wherever Ajax went, oppression rarely survived long - and neither did any walls.

Talon - The Lasting Song
Talon carried the names of the fallen across Fern in song. He sang not only of victory, but of the cost paid to reach it.
Many songs were written about the revolution, but his was the one people sang when they wanted to remember why it had been fought.

Edward - The Untouchable Heartbreaker
Edward slipped easily into the new age, as impossible to catch in peace as he had been in war. Wanted notices, tavern rumors, and love letters followed him in equal number.
He denied most accusations, especially the true ones. The authorities denied employing him, Edward denied being employed, and somehow the guilty kept losing everything anyway.

Quincy - The Bruised Champion
Quincy found little peace after the war, but she found purpose. She became a protector of rebuilding towns, taking the hardest jobs and the heaviest blows without complaint.
Children adored her, soldiers respected her, and enemies quickly learned that Quinn only smiled wider when the fight turned ugly.

Jack - The Last Toast
Jack survived the revolution, surprising his enemies, his allies, and occasionally himself. He later opened a tavern near one of Fern’s busiest roads, where veterans drank cheaply and told stories that grew less believable every year.
Above the door hung a sign reading: “One Last Drink.” No one knew whether it was a promise or a threat.

Shiva - The Trust in Shadows
After the revolution, Shiva returned home to oppose her father, who had left Fern’s ruins behind to continue his struggle for the throne. As the daughter of a fallen crown prince, she was welcomed by few and trusted by even fewer.
From the shadows, she moved against his agents, plans, and loyalists, striking not for power, but to keep her homeland from becoming another realm ruled by fear.
When her father’s men finally cornered her, aid came from Fern - and from the Rangers she had once fought beside. In time, tales spread of a silent blade who changed the fate of her nation without ever claiming its crown.

Corvin - The Everburn Scholar
Corvin’s talents were considered too dangerous by many and too useful by everyone else. He later founded a small workshop devoted to antidotes, controlled flame, and highly questionable safety procedures.
His students remembered his first lesson clearly: poison and medicine differed mostly by dosage - and by whether one’s assistant was paying attention.

Wesley & Sylvia - The General and the Blade
General Wesley helped rebuild Fern’s army, placing loyalty to the people above loyalty to any throne. Under his command, many former soldiers found a way to serve without shame.
Sylvia claimed she stayed near him only because the pay remained good. Years later, when richer contracts came and went, she still had not left.

Salvatore - The Ruined Magnate
Salvatore lost much of his fortune in the revolution and complained about it for the rest of his life. Yet he also invested heavily in roads, trade routes, and reconstruction, insisting there was no profit in a dead country.
Whether he became generous or simply found patriotism profitable remains debated. Salvatore, naturally, encouraged both versions.

Jorah - The Old Smuggler
Jorah remained near the royal survivors until Fern no longer needed old knives in quiet corridors. He then claimed to retire, though few believed him for long.
In the years that followed, supplies often appeared in desperate villages where official roads had failed to reach. No one ever caught the smugglers responsible, but those who knew Jorah noticed he always kept a hidden route out back.
Same as always.

Noah - The Man Who Stopped Running
Noah refused to flee after the final battle. He stood trial, gave testimony against the men who had used him, and asked for no mercy he had not earned.
In time, he found a place among Fern’s border guards. There, he spent the rest of his life proving that freedom was not found by running, but by choosing where to stand.

Marcus - The Friend Who Stood Apart
Marcus fell in the final battle, fighting against those he had once called friends. To many, he became a symbol of the revolution’s deepest wound: not hatred, but love divided by different beliefs.
The Rangers remembered him differently. They remembered his laughter, his loyalty, and the man he had been before conviction led him down another path.
Slyker and Illyana mourned him most of all. Though they could never follow his choice, they never stopped loving the friend they lost.
In the end, Marcus’s death was not remembered as justice. It was remembered as tragedy - proof that even a righteous war could still break the hearts of those who survived it.

THE END
